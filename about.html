About

Introducing JK Overseas Pvt. Ltd.

We are delighted to bring you JK Overseas Pvt. Ltd., a reputable and trustworthy manpower recruitment company established in 2024 under the Company Act 2053, Government of Nepal. Our purpose is clear—to connect Nepalese talent with international employers, creating the ideal match between job requirements and the capabilities of our workforce to achieve optimal results for both.

Our Mission

At JK Overseas, we strive to provide Nepalese workers with meaningful employment opportunities abroad while delivering high-quality, reliable manpower to global companies. By categorizing and preparing our workforce according to industry needs, we ensure that we meet the varied requirements of our international partners.

Our Vision

We envision a future of mutual prosperity where foreign employers achieve growth and success with the support of skilled Nepalese workers, while our employees flourish in their careers. At JK Overseas, we are dedicated to creating these long-term, fulfilling partnerships between employers and job seekers.

Commitment to Quality and Integrity

From the outset, we have focused on offering flawless services through a commitment to integrity, quality, and reliability. We believe that sincerity, diligence, and discipline are key, and our workforce embodies these values. Our goal is to empower our employees with high morale and productivity to help hiring companies thrive.

Ethical and Professional Standards

We take pride in operating with firm ethics and a philanthropic mindset. Our team is highly motivated, approachable, and well-versed in local labor laws, international practices, and the latest industry updates. Equipped with the knowledge and experience necessary to navigate every formal process, we ensure a seamless experience for both employers and employees.

Our Operations

With a centrally located and fully equipped office, we offer hassle-free operations and excellent support, allowing us to efficiently serve our clients and candidates. Every member of our team is committed to upholding high standards and providing the best possible service to our partners abroad.
