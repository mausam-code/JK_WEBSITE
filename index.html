What We Do

We have been successful in supplying skilled, semi-skilled and unskilled workers of diverse fields to various countries outside Nepal. The following categories are available for employment.

Construction

Construction managers, engineers, carpenters, welders, electricians and skilled workers.

Hotels, Restaurants & Catering

Chefs, bakers, waiters, hospitality supervisors and catering staff.

Hyper/Super Markets & Trading

Store managers, sales staff, cashiers and trading personnel.

Security Services

Security supervisors, guards, CCTV controllers and watchmen.

Workshop & Maintenance

Mechanics, technicians, electricians and maintenance workers.

Transportation

Drivers, vehicle operators, mechanics and transportation staff.
